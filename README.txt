RURBANIVE | 360° Botanical Path Tour | standalone map-only intro demo

Open index.html in a browser (Chrome or Edge). No server needed for this map-only demo.
The title and HOW TO USE panels stay visible until you click ENTER TOUR.
The ENTER TOUR button is centered below the hikers and initiates a 2-second zoom into the map.
At the end of the animation, a placeholder appears for Marzipano Part 1. This does not yet launch the 360° tour.

Files: index.html, map.png (original full resolution), rurbanive-logo.png (provided logo).
